# Consolidated Manuscript Development Plan

Use this as a coverage guide, not a fixed chapter-count template. Adapt headings and depth to the project and verified target-journal requirements.

## Project positioning
- Working title and target journal/article type.
- Executive recommendation and confidence.
- Existing evidence versus missing decisive evidence.
- Core scientific question and one central contribution.

## Measurement definitions and comparability
- Definition of every central quantitative metric: measurand, raw signal, method, formula, units, normalization, corrections, uncertainty and source lineage.
- Matched/mismatched/unknown sample and condition dimensions for each material comparison.

## Research frontier and novelty boundary
- Current state of the art and closest competing studies.
- What is known, what is unresolved and why it matters.
- Counter-evidence, scope of search and remaining uncertainty.
- What the project can and cannot claim.

## Introduction
- Paragraph-by-paragraph rhetorical plan.
- Verified literature claims for each technical premise.
- English draft with citation placeholders only where explicitly marked.

## Manuscript storyline
- Ordered scientific argument.
- Each Results/Discussion step and transition logic.
- Claim–Evidence relationships.

## Visual manuscript architecture
- TOC/graphical abstract brief, adapted to journal requirements.
- Main Figures in story order, each with panel-level content and data provenance.
- Supporting Information map linked to main claims and Figures.

## Experimental and analytical work packages
- Existing-data recovery and validation tasks.
- New experiments and controls.
- Quantitative analysis, uncertainty/statistics and acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies, priorities, blockers and decision gates.

## Theory and computational work packages
- Scientific question, system/model, inputs, methods, observables and validation.
- Handoff-ready task descriptions.
- Explicit blocked items and prerequisites.

## Project execution
- Milestones and sequencing justified by dependencies.
- Student-facing self-check.
- Supervisor decisions required.
- Risks and stop/redirect conditions.

## Journal adaptation and submission preparation
- Dated official-source profile.
- Verified formatting/structural requirements.
- Unresolved instructions that must be checked again before submission.

## References and provenance
- Full bibliography with verification level.
- Source inventory, Evidence IDs and calculation lineage.
- Validation report and unresolved warnings.
