## 6. Atomic novelty-proposition stress test

| Candidate contribution proposition | Search variants used to challenge it | Closest prior-art source(s) | What is already established | Observable consequence required to establish a difference | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Assessment: ALREADY_ESTABLISHED / INCREMENTAL_EXTENSION / POTENTIALLY_DISTINCT / NOT_ASSESSABLE.

A new combination of known factors is not automatically novel. Explain the non-trivial interaction, quantitative relationship, prediction or previously unresolved boundary that would make it scientifically meaningful.

Keep conflicting evidence visible. Do not call a paper a direct counterexample if its system, reaction direction, conditions or measured quantity are materially different; explain its proper role as a boundary case or adjacent comparator.
