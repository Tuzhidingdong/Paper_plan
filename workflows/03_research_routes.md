# 03 — Research Routes (specified; not implemented in MVP)

Generate three genuinely different scientific argument routes from validated Findings and literature gaps. Each route states central question, candidate novelty, bounded gap, existing Result Units, evidence limitations, sample mapping, competing literature, alternative artifact explanations, hypothesis status, Data Slots, additional tasks, feasibility and novelty risk.

A route may be exploratory if support is weak. Do not manufacture a third mature route just to satisfy a count.

Human Gate A supports select, optimize, replace, merge, request new literature search or defer. Persist the approval decision. Framework planning cannot proceed until a human-approved route exists.
