# Figure and Supporting Information design reference

## Main-text Figure
Each Figure should answer one scientific question and make one principal point. Plan only the panels needed to support that point.

A panel specification records:
- panel purpose and content;
- source evidence or missing Data Slot;
- sample/condition/control comparisons;
- units, statistics and uncertainty where relevant;
- visualization type and acceptance criteria;
- claim supported and interpretation limit.

Do not impose a fixed Figure or Panel count. The target journal and story determine the final number.

## TOC / graphical abstract
Check the current article-type requirement first. Communicate the central contribution with a simple visual sequence. Clearly distinguish measured outcome, supported interpretation and hypothesis.

## Supporting Information
SI supports reproducibility, extended evidence and secondary controls. Link every SI item to a claim, Figure or method. Do not hide evidence essential to the central contribution in SI.
