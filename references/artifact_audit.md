# Artifact and confounder audit

Before promoting an observation to an interpretation, consider alternatives.

Universal checks: sample identity, preparation differences, instrument calibration, acquisition settings, background/baseline/smoothing/fitting, normalization and units, replicates, controls, alternate mechanisms, image cropping/contrast, and selection bias.

XRD examples: sample height/displacement, zero shift, preferred orientation, peak overlap, background fitting, scan range/step/time, stacking offsets.

XPS examples: charge referencing, background and fit constraints, raw points versus fit envelope, contamination, sampling depth, charging and non-unique fits.

Catalysis examples: catalyst mass/loading, active-area or BET normalization, initial concentration, pH, temperature, light intensity, mass transfer, dark adsorption, blank controls, product calibration and rate basis.

Electrochemistry examples: electrode area/loading, reference electrode, electrolyte, iR compensation, scan rate, geometric versus mass-specific current, cell configuration and replicate variability.

For each factor record relevance, what the report shows, provenance, plausible alternative explanation and what would resolve it. Missing metadata does not prove the result is wrong.
