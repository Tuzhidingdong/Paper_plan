# Journal adaptation reference

Journal requirements are volatile external facts. Store them per project, with dated source references.

## Verification hierarchy
1. Current official author instructions for the relevant article type.
2. Current publisher/journal pages for figures, graphical abstracts, SI and policies.
3. Official submission checklists or templates.
4. Secondary guidance only when official information is inaccessible; label it secondary and unresolved.

## Required behavior
- Record exact URL, access date, the item inspected and relevant section.
- Do not infer a requirement from another article type or another journal in the same publisher family.
- Do not freeze word limits, figure limits, reference styles, resolution requirements or policies into generic Skill instructions.
- Mark every not-found or ambiguous requirement as unknown.
- Recheck before submission.
- Assess fit qualitatively against scope, readership, article type, contribution, evidence maturity and novelty risk. Never promise acceptance.

## Project record
Use journal-source IDs in the Manuscript Development Bundle. The profile should distinguish verified requirements, provisional recommendations and unresolved items.
